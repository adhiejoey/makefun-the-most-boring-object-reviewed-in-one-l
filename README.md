One line reviewing the most boring object on every screen: the blinking text
cursor, a thin black line that has spent forty years flashing "your turn" at
humans who type three letters, delete four, then wander off for coffee
mid-sentence. The joke is the cursor's own dullness — it waits forever, does
nothing but blink, and is the one piece of the machine that watches us fail to
have a thought.

## How it works

`main.ts` writes the review to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default
`/output`), with a guard that the line stays within the 200-character gate.
